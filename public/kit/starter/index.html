Agenda

Two hours, four moves

01 · Load a raw asset

Start with CAD geometry that has no business loading in a browser. Watch it load anyway.

02 · Wire the SDK

One script tag and a scene id. We'll read the network panel together and talk about what isn't there.

03 · Adaptive fidelity

How the same scene serves a phone and a workstation without you branching on device.

04 · Measure it

Time to first view, on your machine, against a Draco baseline. Bring your own asset if you have one.
